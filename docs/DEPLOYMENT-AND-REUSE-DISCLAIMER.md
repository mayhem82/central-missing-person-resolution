# Deployment and Departmental Reuse Disclaimer

This repository contains a public-interest disaster affected-person reconciliation deployment and the underlying implementation used to operate it.

Government departments, emergency services, disaster-management authorities, hospitals, humanitarian organisations, local authorities and other legitimate public-interest organisations may review this repository and adapt the deployment for their own incident-response requirements, subject to their own legal, privacy, security, records-management, accessibility, procurement and operational obligations.

This deployment is not an official government system unless and until it is formally adopted, operated or endorsed by the relevant authority. Its records are evidence-linked source claims and reconciliation outputs, not substitutes for authoritative agency determinations. Absence from the system does not establish that a person was unaffected, located or never reported. Presence in a source list does not by itself establish current status, identity, safety, injury or death.

Any organisation reusing this deployment should preserve the core evidence controls: immutable source snapshots, provenance, separation of source records from canonical people, no name-only identity merges, human review of consequential identity decisions, append-only status history, explicit uncertainty, and controlled handling of sensitive personal information.

Departments wishing to deploy their own version should fork or copy the repository, replace the incident-specific source manifest and configuration, review the privacy and operating rules, validate source access and parser behaviour, and conduct their own security and legal review before operational use.
