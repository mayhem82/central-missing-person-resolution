# Operating Rules

1. A source record is a claim about a person, not the person itself.
2. Preserve original language, spelling, formatting and source reference before normalisation.
3. Never merge on name alone.
4. A candidate link and a confirmed identity are different objects.
5. `CONFIRMED_MATCH` requires a hard bridge and human review.
6. Status changes are append-only events; never erase earlier states.
7. Removal from a later list does not by itself mean located or safe.
8. Continued appearance on a missing list does not by itself prove continued disappearance when source lists are not reconciled.
9. `OUT_OF_CONTACT` does not imply swept away, injured or deceased.
10. Public views must minimise private contact information and unresolved identifying detail.
11. Automated logic may flag candidates and conflicts but must not determine death or consequential identity autonomously.
12. Every material conclusion must remain traceable to its source record(s).
