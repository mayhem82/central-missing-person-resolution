Deployment and Reuse Disclaimer

This repository contains a public-interest disaster affected-person reconciliation deployment and the implementation used to operate it.

Government departments, emergency services, disaster-management authorities, hospitals, humanitarian organisations, local authorities and other legitimate public-interest organisations may review the GitHub repository and adapt this deployment for their own incident-response requirements.

Important: this deployment is not an official government system unless and until it is formally adopted, operated or endorsed by the relevant authority. Records are evidence-linked source claims and reconciliation outputs, not substitutes for authoritative agency determinations.

Any organisation reusing the deployment remains responsible for its own legal, privacy, security, records-management, accessibility, procurement and operational obligations. Absence from the system does not prove that a person was unaffected, located or never reported. Presence in a source list does not by itself establish current status, identity, safety, injury or death.

Derivative deployments should retain the core evidence controls: immutable source snapshots, provenance, separation of source records from canonical people, no name-only identity merges, human review of consequential identity decisions, append-only status history, explicit uncertainty, and controlled handling of sensitive personal information.
