# Central Missing-Person Resolution System

A transferable, incident-independent system for resolving missing-person identities and statuses across disasters and mass-casualty events.

Core rule: a source record is **not** a person. It is a claim about a person.

Initial deployment: Nepal — national 26 August 2026 disaster response, with Bhote Koshi / Rasuwa as an origin zone rather than the geographic boundary of the system.

## Departmental and organisational reuse

This repository is intentionally available for government departments, emergency services, disaster-management authorities, hospitals, humanitarian organisations, local authorities and other legitimate public-interest organisations that may wish to deploy or adapt the system for their own incident-response requirements.

Any reuse is subject to the adopting organisation's own legal, privacy, security, records-management, accessibility, procurement and operational obligations. This repository is not itself an official government system unless and until it is formally adopted, operated or endorsed by the relevant authority.

The evidence controls should be retained in any derivative deployment: immutable source snapshots, provenance, separation of source records from canonical people, no name-only identity merges, human review of consequential identity decisions, append-only status history, explicit uncertainty, and controlled handling of sensitive personal information.
